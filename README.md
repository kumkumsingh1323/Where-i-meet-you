# Where Did I Meet You?

## Remember who you met. Remember why it mattered.

**Where Did I Meet You?** is a personal relationship memory system that helps people remember the people they meet, where they met them, what they discussed, and why the connection mattered.

### Live Demo

https://intelligent-recall-every-connection.base44.app

## Problem

People meet others through conferences, networking events, work, travel, workshops, business meetings, social gatherings and online communities. Later, they often forget who someone was, where they met, what they discussed, and why they connected.

Traditional contact apps mainly store names and contact details. This project focuses on the **context behind relationships**.

## Solution

The application creates a searchable personal memory of relationships.

Users can save:
- People
- Events
- Meetings
- Locations
- Conversations
- Interests
- Skills
- Companies
- Tags
- Follow-ups
- Photos

Users can later search these memories using natural-language questions.

Example:

> Who was the person I met at the AI event who works on healthcare?

The application can find the most relevant saved memory and show the context behind the connection.

## Key Features

### Natural-Language Memory Search
Search questions such as:
- Where did I meet Rahul?
- Who did I meet at the AI conference?
- Who works in healthcare?
- What did Priya and I discuss?
- Who did I meet in Hyderabad last month?
- Who haven't I contacted recently?
- Show people interested in startups.

### People Management
Store:
- Name and profile photo
- Job and company
- Role
- Email and phone
- Location
- Social links
- Interests and skills
- Tags
- Notes
- Relationship type

### Memory Capture
Record:
- Person
- Event
- Date
- Location
- How you met
- Conversation
- Topics
- Notes
- Photos
- Tags
- Follow-up date

### Relationship Timeline
View the history of a relationship chronologically.

### Events
Create events such as AI meetups, conferences, workshops, business meetings, weddings, travel and networking events.

### Follow-Up Reminders
Schedule reminders to reconnect with people.

### Connection Insights
View people met, industries, interests, events, locations and people needing follow-up.

### Privacy
Users should only have access to their own people, memories, events, notes, photos and follow-ups.

## Why This Is Different

This project is not designed as:
- A basic contact book
- A CRM
- A generic notes application
- A social media platform
- A normal calendar

The core idea is:

> **People are memories, not just contacts.**

The application focuses on remembering the **context behind a relationship**, rather than simply storing contact information.
